The Problem We're Solving

Healthcare management at the ground level operates in the dark.

❌ No Real-Time Monitoring

No unified mechanism to know the live status of PHCs. Issues are discovered days after they happen.

❌ Doctor Absenteeism

Without secure attendance systems, proxy attendance and unexplained absences remain undetected.

❌ Poor Data for Decisions

Manual reporting means district-level decisions are based on outdated or completely inaccurate information.

Platform Features

Everything required for complete healthcare oversight.

Live PHC Tracking
